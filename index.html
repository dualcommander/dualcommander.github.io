Familiar tools. Focused workflow.

Files side by side

Copy, move, rename, and delete across two panes, with background progress and cancellation. Compare source and target details before choosing Skip, Overwrite, or applying either choice to all conflicts in that operation. Deletions use Trash or Recycle Bin by default.

Keyboard first

Switch panes with Tab. Copy with F5, move with F6, create files with Shift+F4, and create folders with F7. Mark files individually or as a range; swap panes or open the same folder on both sides.

Look inside archives

Browse ZIP, TAR, 7z, RAR, and compressed files, including password-protected ZIP, 7z, and RAR. Browsing is read-only; nested archives are not browsed in-pane. Opening a member creates a temporary copy (up to 256 MiB), removed on exit. Edits do not update the archive.

At home on your OS

Open files in your default apps, edit in an external text editor, reveal files, copy paths, or launch a terminal in the current folder. Right-click for Open With and Get Info on macOS, or Open With and Properties on Windows.

Your view, remembered

Brief and detailed views, sorting, shared favourite folders, light and dark themes, adjustable density, and font sizes. Pane paths and window state persist.

Stay up to date

Folder changes refresh automatically through native OS notifications. Find files by name or content, and switch between mounted volumes.

Platform requirements

  • macOS: Apple Silicon (arm64) and Intel (amd64), as DMG or ZIP. Open the DMG and drag DualCommander into Applications.
  • Windows: amd64.
  • Linux: amd64, as DEB, RPM, or tar.xz. Requires glibc 2.35+ and a desktop session with OpenGL support; system graphics libraries are not bundled.

Linux compatibility targets include Debian 12+, Ubuntu 22.04+, Mint 21+, and compatible Fedora/RHEL-family releases, not RHEL 9 or older. These are targets, not a tested distro matrix. See the installation guide for package commands and requirements.

macOS and Windows packages are unsigned and not notarized. Your OS may show security warnings. Download only from the project repository and review those warnings before running.
